# Long-horizon scenarios

Weeks of virtual time in one case: scripted people and agents act on what they were asked (`reply`, `policy`, `work`),
the bot gets its ticks every virtual hour and reacts. Scored by outcomes (`type = "outcome"`): share of the project
done, messages to the principal, and whether anything went past its due date without the principal hearing first.

Run with `bot-connect eval run --suite long`. They take tens of minutes each.
